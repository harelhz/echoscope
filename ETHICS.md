# Ethics and responsible use

Echoscope is a tool for understanding and countering coordinated manipulation of public
discourse. Tools like this can be misused, so these principles are part of the project.

## What Echoscope is for

- Researchers, journalists and fact-checkers investigating influence operations
- Trust-and-safety and threat-intelligence teams
- Analysts studying how narratives spread across public channels

## Design principles

1. **Public data only.** Collectors read public channels through official APIs. Echoscope
   never joins private groups, reads private messages, or bypasses access controls.
2. **Channels, not people.** Analysis is about channel behaviour (timing, copying,
   co-posting). Echoscope doesn't attempt to identify, locate or profile the individuals
   behind accounts, and features that do so won't be accepted.
3. **Leads, not verdicts.** A score means a pattern is worth a human look. Coordination can
   be benign (news agencies, official statements, fan communities). Verify before you
   publish or act.
4. **Explainability.** Every score breaks down into named signals so findings can be
   checked, challenged and reproduced.
5. **Minimal data.** Images are reduced to perceptual hashes and not stored. Store only what
   an investigation needs, and delete it when you're done.

## Please don't use Echoscope to

- Target, harass or surveil journalists, activists, minorities or private individuals
- Suppress legitimate speech or organising
- Violate platform terms of service or applicable law, including data protection law

## Reporting misuse or risks

If you find a way Echoscope could cause harm, please open an issue or contact the
maintainers privately.
